# Commercial Model

Free diagnoses; paid fixes and rehearses. A free candidate sees exactly where they are weak for their target role, but needs the paid tier to fix it and practise until it holds. Agreed on 13 October 2026 (D-20); prices and caps remain subject to the validation tests below.

## The line in the sand

The line sits between knowing and fixing. Free gives the candidate an honest, accurate diagnosis of their readiness for one target role. Paid gives them the means to close the gaps and rehearse until the answers hold. The candidate feels the shortfall because the free tier is specific about what is wrong, not because it is deliberately poor.

| Pain moment | What free shows | What paid unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence scoring | Every CV achievement scored 1 to 7, weak ones flagged | Full evidence sprint to strengthen all of them (free covers the first 3) |
| Completeness probing | Completeness view and the first 3 probe questions | Every probe until the input is well rounded |
| Fit/gap heat map | The full heat map, including uncovered must-haves | Gap Stories for every unmatched must-have (D-37), plus the full story portfolio (free names 2 stories) |
| Question bank | The top 5 predicted questions and the total count ("42 predicted questions") | All questions with reasons, probes and linked stories |
| Diagnostic mock | One 5-question mock with dimension scores and one priority fix | Fixes on every answer, re-attempts, follow-up probes, unlimited mocks in every format |
| One-way video | One practice question with real timer settings | Full simulation of the invitation's question set |
| Drill deck | 10 cards in a single session | Full deck, missed cards returning across sessions, three drill levels |
| Readiness view | "Not ready" with the specific uncovered must-haves | The path to "ready": schedule, cue card and interview brief |

## Tiers and proposed prices

The lead paid product is a one-off Interview Sprint, because interview preparation is episodic: candidates prepare for a date, then stop. A subscription serves repeat interviewers such as contractors. Prices are set in NZD for the NZ launch (D-21), using these USD reference points, anchored to competitors (Big Interview $39 for 30 days; Yoodli Pro $8 to $20 a month; Final Round AI about $25 a month on annual billing).

| Tier | Who it is for | What it includes | Proposed price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free (Explore) | Anyone with a target role | One active role; full role decoding and benchmark; capped diagnosis (see pain moments) | Free |
| Interview Sprint | A candidate with an interview coming | One role, 30 days, everything: full organisation brief, probing, story portfolio, all mock formats, drills, cue card, debrief | US$39 one-off, no auto-renewal |
| Pro | Contractors and active job seekers interviewing repeatedly | Everything in Sprint for unlimited roles; achievement inventory reused across applications | US$24 a month or US$199 a year |
| Organisation licences | Universities, outplacement firms, recruiters, public sector agencies | Bulk Sprint or Pro codes for their candidates; no organisation users in the platform (D-10) | Volume discount on Sprint and Pro |

Framework packs (D-18) are included in Sprint and Pro when released, not sold separately, so no candidate pays more because of where they work (P10).

## Upgrade moments

The offer appears at the point of highest intent, tied to the specific shortfall the candidate has just seen, never as a general nag.

| Moment | Trigger | Offer shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| After the diagnostic mock | Mock results displayed | "Fix all 5 answers and practise until they score 5" (Sprint) |
| Gaps on the heat map | Uncovered must-haves visible | "Get a planned answer for each gap" (Sprint) |
| Interview is close | Interview date within 14 days | Sprint, timed to finish before the date |
| One-way video invitation | Video settings entered | Full simulation of the invitation (Sprint) |
| Probe or drill cap reached | Third probe answered, or tenth card drilled | Continue where they stopped (Sprint) |
| Second target role | Candidate adds another role | Pro, with the inventory carried over |

## Guardrails

The shortfall must come from scope, never from lowered quality or pressure tactics, because trust is the product's positioning (P7, P8).

- Everything shown free is accurate and complete in itself. Scores and diagnoses are never degraded to push an upgrade.
- Locked items show what they contain and how many, never fake or blurred previews.
- No countdown timers, fake scarcity or guilt wording.
- Sprint never auto-renews; Pro cancels in one step.
- Data access, export and deletion are free for everyone (LR-05).
- Paid tiers never promise offers or score uplifts (P8).

## Assumptions to validate

Plan on a 3% free-to-paid conversion and treat anything above 8% as exceptional. Across 200 products the median is 8%, but 3% to 5% counts as good for self-serve freemium and 8% to 12% as great ([ChartMogul, 2026](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report/)). Traditional freemium averages 3.7%, with EdTech, the closest category, at 2.6% ([Userpilot, citing First Page Sage](https://userpilot.com/blog/freemium-to-premium)).

- [ ] Price test the Sprint with NZ candidates at the NZD equivalents of US$29, US$39 and US$49 on a landing page before build.
- [ ] Model AI cost per mock, probe and video session, and set free caps so a free user's cost stays below a target per head.
- [ ] Measure activation (diagnostic mock completed) and conversion within 14 days of sign-up.
- [ ] Check that the interview-date trigger lifts conversion, as the episodic model predicts.
- [ ] Confirm organisations will buy codes without in-platform reporting, given no organisation users (D-10).
